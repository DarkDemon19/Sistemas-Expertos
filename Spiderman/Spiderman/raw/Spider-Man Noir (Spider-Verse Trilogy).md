**Spider-Man Noir** (real name **Peter Parker**) is a private eye, and a major protagonist in the 2016 film *Spider-Man: Into The Spider-Verse*. He is a dark and monochromatic version of Peter Parker from a universe where it is 1933. Like his teammates, he was bitten by a radioactive spider and became a detective paying homage to noir films. Dragged into Miles Morales' homeworld by Kingpin, Spider-Man Noir joins forces with his fellow Spider-Men of the Multiverse to defeat Kingpin and return to his own universe.
